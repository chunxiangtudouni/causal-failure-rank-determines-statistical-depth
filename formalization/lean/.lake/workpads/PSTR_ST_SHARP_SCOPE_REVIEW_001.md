strict/public/all-owned acceptance. At writing, final gate035 is still active.

The target remains the unchanged `CompactPrice.SharpUpperClaims` in
`PaperProofs/Common/CompactPriceClaims.lean`. Source036's theorem takes no
arguments beyond inhabiting this original Prop. Its final policy uses the
original bounded TABLE rule, original quota/exponent, original native mode,
original complete-H0 qualification and original symbolic price expression.

epsilon and cutoff; delta, risk budget, original contracts and accounting

which depends only on the fixed table instance and availability. The two

derives that a genuine second-stage entry either used no tools or exactly
